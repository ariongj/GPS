      Multi-constellation

      More satellites overhead, a tighter fix on the ground.

      Each tracker listens to GPS, Galileo, GLONASS and BeiDou together. Between tall buildings, under bridges and along valley roads, that means enough satellites to keep a confident fix where single-system receivers drift.

      Geofences
