      Questions? Answers.
      
      
      
        
      Do I need to buy trackers?
      Yes, one per vehicle. Each tracker is €89, or included on a 3-year plan. Fitting takes about 30 minutes, and our partners can do it at your depot.
      
        
      What happens in tunnels or without mobile signal?
      The tracker keeps recording. It stores up to 72 hours of driving and uploads it in order as soon as the signal returns, so there are no gaps in the history.
      
        
      Can drivers have privacy outside working hours?
      Yes. With privacy mode on, trips outside the hours you set are recorded as distance only, without location.
      
        
      Where is our data stored?
      In data centres inside the EU. It's encrypted in transit and at rest, and you choose how long it's kept.
      
        
      Can we try it before we commit?
      Yes. We send three trackers for a free 30-day trial. If it isn't right for you, send them back.
